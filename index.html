hub Shared Vision Layer Architecture (/vision)

Both games run on identical frozen interfaces, sharing vision tracking and evidence generation.

/vision/poseTracker.js

Wraps MediaPipe Pose Landmarker in video and still image modes. Tracks up to 4 individuals simultaneously at 15 FPS reduced resolution.

/vision/motion.js

Scale-invariant motion metric evaluated on 8 body joints divided by shoulder width. Includes 6s calibration routine.

/vision/faceMatch.js

Wraps Vladimir Mandic Human library. Memory enrollment and cosine similarity matching with normalized bounding boxes.
